# Brand rationale | Flying Buttress 2.0

## Keep the name. Refine the meaning.

The historic flying buttress is a clever structural solution that allows a building to do more with less material in the way. It supports ambitious architecture while staying largely out of the creative spotlight. That is a useful metaphor for technology at design practices: complex competence in service of exceptional work.

## Core promise

**Technology for the way you design.**

## Positioning

A sector-focused technology partner offering managed IT, cybersecurity, cloud collaboration, technology strategy and emerging technology guidance for architecture, engineering and design practices.

## Brand personality

- Design-literate, not flashy
- Direct, candid, experienced
- Calmly confident in complexity
- Curious about the future without being credulous
- Business-minded and collaborative

## Visual principles

1. **Architecture as grammar:** grids, sections, framed space, structural balance and generous negative space.
2. **Material contrast:** dark structural navy against warm limestone, with terracotta used as a deliberate accent.
3. **Editorial hierarchy:** very large headlines, disciplined typography and modest annotation text inspired by architectural drawings.
4. **The work comes first:** interfaces should guide buyers to specific outcomes and services, rather than displaying a long equipment/software list.
5. **Real evidence:** testimonials, named sources and carefully explained outcomes, without invented metrics.
