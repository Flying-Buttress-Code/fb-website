Our story

Born inside the
design world.

Flying Buttress was founded around a simple conviction: an architecture or engineering firm deserves IT people who understand what makes it different.

Designed for design firms
01 / Why we exist

Not another
generic MSP.

Founder Michael Donahue spent years leading technology inside an architecture practice before starting Flying Buttress in 2010. He knew the difference between a provider who understands help-desk software and one who understands what is at stake on a deadline day in a design studio.

His goal was to build the kind of technology partner he had wanted to hire: knowledgeable, candid, reliable and more interested in helping existing clients succeed than chasing the next contract.

That remains the point of view behind Flying Buttress today.

The way we think

Three kinds
of understanding.

01 / TECHNICAL

Know the systems.

AED application stacks, high-performance devices, demanding files, global project collaboration, and the infrastructure behind them.

02 / FINANCIAL

Know the business.

Technology is a business decision. Budgets, project economics, risk and the cost of interrupted work all matter.

03 / CULTURAL

Know the people.

Architecture and engineering are collaborative disciplines. Technology changes need to earn support, not just arrive as mandates.
