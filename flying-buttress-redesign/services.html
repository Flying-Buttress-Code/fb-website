Services

The tech is complex.
The partnership isn't.

From the help desk to the leadership table, Flying Buttress provides the specialized knowledge and practical support that design firms need.

“Built around the actual work of architecture, engineering and design.
01 / An integrated approach

Not six silos.
One clear strategy.

Services work better when they work together. Hardware choices affect Revit performance. File systems affect collaboration. Cybersecurity affects delivery deadlines. We connect those decisions instead of treating them as separate tickets.

Whether you need fully managed IT or a capable partner to work with your internal team, we start with the needs of the firm, not a prescribed technology stack.

What we bring to the table
01.

Managed IT

Day-to-day technology should be almost invisible. We manage the systems, support the people and resolve problems before they consume valuable project time.

  • Workstations and mobile devices
  • End-user help desk
  • Infrastructure health and monitoring
  • Onboarding and offboarding
02.

Cloud & collaboration

Large models, multiple offices and tight delivery schedules require more than a shared drive. We plan cloud and hybrid systems around how teams actually access and exchange project data.

  • Cloud and hybrid migrations
  • Large-file access and synchronization
  • Microsoft 365 and Azure
  • Autodesk Construction Cloud and Egnyte environments
03.

Cybersecurity & resilience

Protect the work without getting in its way. We help design firms manage security risk, recovery readiness and the human realities of an open, collaborative profession.

  • Security reviews and planning
  • Identity and endpoint protections
  • Backup and business continuity
  • Security awareness and recovery planning
04.

Strategic IT leadership

Technology belongs in the business conversation. Our advisory services connect systems decisions to cash flow, operations, budgets and long-term resilience.

  • Technology roadmap development
  • Fractional and virtual CIO guidance
  • IT budgeting and investment review
  • Support for growth and multi-office change
05.

AED applications & workflows

The software and data behind project delivery are specialized. We understand the implications of BIM, CAD, visualization, licensing and document management on the entire environment.

  • Autodesk Revit and AutoCAD environments
  • Bluebeam and visualization workflows
  • Design application administration
  • Performance and licensing planning
06.

AI & emerging technology

AI is creating possibilities, and plenty of noise. We help leadership teams evaluate practical use cases, data readiness, governance and the real costs of adopting new capabilities.

  • AI opportunity and risk discovery
  • Data access and governance planning
  • Use-case assessment and pilots
  • Technology change management
Co-managed by design

Have an internal IT team? We can work alongside them.

We bring additional capacity, specialist AED experience, and strategic perspective without requiring an all-or-nothing outsourcing decision.
